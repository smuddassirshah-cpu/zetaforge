## What is certified

Everything below is enforced by a test in the default ctest suite, in both
Release and Debug, with identical determinism hashes across the two.

- **Outward-rounding radius primitives** (`core/include/zetaforge/radius.hpp`,
  MATHS.md D7). `up_add`, `up_mul`, `inflate` and `half_ulp_bound` return a
  value greater than or equal to the exact real result on non-negative
  operands, at every magnitude including binade boundaries, the subnormal
  range and the approach to overflow. Soundness is enforced structurally by a
  bit-exact integer reference (`test_radius`, `exact_ref.hpp`) that shares no
  derivation with the implementation, not by statistical comparison: the
  reference derives its answer from the bit-length identity and integer unit
  counts, so it cannot reproduce a normalisation bug.
- **Ball arithmetic** (`ball.hpp`, `ball.cpp`). Centre in mpfr at a fixed
  working precision, radius as a non-negative double. `add`, `sub`, `mul`,
  `scale` and `parse` enclose. No binary operation claims exactness, and no
  inexact value is ever given a zero radius.
- **Fixed-point type** (`ffix.hpp`) with tracked error inflation that cannot
  under-report, and multiplication overflow decided BEFORE assembly rather
  than by inspecting an already-wrapped value.
- **Exact NTT convolution** (`ntt.hpp`) over p = 998244353, cross-checked
  against schoolbook convolution at boundary lengths. Fitted cost slope 1.111
  with R^2 = 0.99995 over n = 2^10..2^20, single-threaded on an Apple M2;
  conditions recorded in docs/benchmarks/ntt-bench.md.
- **Cross-configuration determinism**: `test_determinism` runs a fixed
  operation sequence and prints DETERMINISM_HASH; CI requires the Release and
  Debug hashes to be identical, which is what makes `-ffp-contract=off`
  load-bearing rather than decorative.

## What the falsifiability apparatus shows

docs/gate/ATTACKS.md rows 1, 13 and 14 mutate this kernel and require the
suite to fail. tools/gate_battery.sh runs them from clean builds and the CI leg
"gate-battery" runs it on every push to main.

## What is open

- **Review finding B7 (MINOR), not closed.** `convolve_cyclic` validates
  lengths but not residue range: inputs at or above the modulus silently
  produce wrong residues. The companion half of B7, a magnitude ceiling taken
  with `fabs` after a directed conversion, was fixed at rev 5.
- Two enclosure defects were found in this stage AFTER it was approved
  (`Ball::parse` deriving its radius from the double image of the centre, and
  `Ffix::mul` wrapping silently), by the independent readiness review of
  2026-08-24. Both were repaired at rev 5 and both now have a ledger row. An
  approved stage is not a proven one; it is a stage nobody has yet broken.
