## What is certified

`theta_certified(t, prec)` returns a ball containing the true

    theta(t) = arg Gamma(1/4 + it/2) - (t/2) ln pi

for every t >= t0 = 200, with a radius carrying three explicit components
(MATHS.md D1): the series truncation remainder, mpfr rounding at the working
precision, and the representation error of the coefficients parsed at that
precision.

- **Coefficients.** c_1..c_6 are exact rationals, held as integer numerator
  and denominator pairs and parsed at working precision, never as doubles.
  They agree exactly with the classical closed form for the theta asymptotic
  coefficients; c_6 = 1414477/1476034560 and the first omitted magnitude is
  c_7 = 8191/2555904.
- **Validity threshold.** t0 = 200 is pinned from Gabcke 1979 Thm 1 p.139 as
  quoted in Arias de Reyna, Math. Comp. 80 (2011), NOT from observed
  agreement (MATHS.md D2). The working precision of 128 bits is derived from
  the phase accuracy the campaign needs at height 3x10^12, not chosen.
- **Oracle.** A committed mpmath corpus (docs/golden/theta_golden.csv,
  ~170 significant digits), compared in full mpfr precision with the tolerance
  set to the certified radius exactly and no additive slack. 84 combinations,
  21 heights by 4 precisions.
- **Policy equality.** The radius is compared against an independent
  transcription of the whole derivation, so production drift breaks the suite
  even where the corpus cannot see it.

## What is open

(Everything in this section describes the tree AT commit 665f94c, which is
what the tag points at. The O1 item below was closed at stage 4 rev 7 by
MATHS.md D1b, which proves the remainder bound and retires the factor 4; the
paragraph is preserved because it is true of the tagged commit.)

- **MATHS.md O1, the load-bearing item.** The D1 remainder bound is
  empirically calibrated, not proven. It claims
  `4 * (c7 + c8/t^2) / t^13`, and the factor 4 is NOT headroom: the true
  remainder strictly exceeds the unscaled bound at every height, because the
  omitted tail is a sum of positive terms (measured ratio 1.0000000175 at
  t = 200; review finding A3). Closing O1 means transcribing Gabcke's explicit
  per-term critical-line constants. A Stirling sector bound will not do it:
  sector constants at these orders far exceed 4. Until O1 closes, the theta
  safety factor is a load-bearing assumption, and stage 6's verifier
  specification ("re-derives sign logic from published theta bounds") rests on
  it.
- **Below t0 this function throws.** The Euler-Maclaurin path owns
  0 < t < 200 and did not exist at this release; MATHS.md D8 is where the
  certified sub-t0 derivation goes.
- Detection of a weakened theta radius currently runs through the policy
  transcription (ATTACKS.md row 2), which is an independent transcription of
  the same derivation rather than an independent enclosure. A radius wrong in
  both would survive. Recorded as an accepted blind spot with its owning work.

## What was retracted

The reported FLINT `acb_lgamma` anomaly (MATHS.md O2) was a measurement
artefact, not a library defect: interval midpoints were read through double
conversions against full-precision references. Independent re-testing on
2026-08-24 reconfirmed that `acb_lgamma` is rigorous. Nothing here is
reportable upstream. The consequence stands on its own merits: mpmath goldens
are the theta oracle of record.
