# zetaforge readiness review, 2026-08-24

Independent full-repository review: plan scope, current state, potential, and
defects not recorded in the project's own ledgers. Produced outside the gating
protocol as commissioned analysis; it modifies no stage code and no tracker
state. Method: six independent specialist review passes (theta mathematics,
EM/RS mathematics, ball arithmetic, test integrity, protocol and state
integrity, plan and strategy), each running its own builds and experiments,
followed by direct reproduction of every load-bearing claim. Findings whose
evidence was reproduced first-hand during synthesis are marked [verified];
findings resting on a reviewer's recorded experiment are marked [measured].

## 1. Verdict

The project is real and the culture is unusually good: the attack-ledger
discipline, the rev-1 corrections, and the radius kernel are genuine. But the
tree is not where its tracker says it is, and the parts added most recently
are the weakest. Three summary judgements:

1. Stage 4 must not pass review in its current state. Its status row says
   "awaiting review" while zero of its three definition-of-done items have
   working code behind them, and the mathematical plan it rests on (MATHS.md
   D8) is provably incapable of delivering the gamma_1 reproduction the gate
   requires.
2. The falsifiability apparatus has quietly rotted. The production radius
   sabotage channel is dead (the build that enables it does not configure),
   one sabotage test is hard-coded to pass, the stage-4 test suite is green
   with zero functional coverage, and the recorded stage-3 verification
   battery is not reproducible from this tree.
3. The plan is better than most research plans but prices the wrong
   algorithm, leaves the certified-error analysis of its chosen multipoint
   method (the hardest open problem in the whole programme) implicit, and
   depends on a gate reviewer no document defines.

Nothing found invalidates a value the engine has actually certified: the
theta coefficient table, the golden corpus, and the stage-2 radius primitives
all survived independent adversarial checking. The rot is in the new complex
ball layer, the stage-4 plan, the test theatre around them, and the record.

frontier (H0 = 3,000,175,332,800) by a window D, baseline 10^11, stretch
10^12, releasing every zero ordinate with machine-checkable certificates and

bias. The scope derivation, the honest GPU retraction in r2.1, the

The scope problems that survive scrutiny are in section 5, group E.

## 5. Findings register

Deduplicated from 95 raw findings across the six passes. Severity: CRITICAL

### A. Certification mathematics

- A1 CRITICAL [verified]. MATHS.md D8 cannot certify gamma_1. Since
  Z(t) = e^{i theta(t)} zeta(1/2+it) and |e^{i theta}| = 1, |zeta| carries no
  sign information: |Z| = |zeta| identically. D8's plan ("use |zeta| as a
  lower bound for enclosure purposes below t0") therefore cannot certify the
  sign flip that defines gamma_1; the pre-registered L-B bracket (Z(14)
  negative, Z(15) positive) is unsatisfiable, since both heights return
  positive magnitudes. The declared interface already embeds the confusion:
  ZResult carries only "Ball re". D8's own formula "Z(t)=Re[e^{i theta}]zeta"
  parses as cos(theta)*zeta, which is complex-valued and wrong. What is
  needed below t0 is a certified enclosure of theta(t) itself (arg Gamma on
  the principal branch, e.g. Gamma recurrence into Stirling validity with the
  explicit Binet remainder), then Z = u cos(theta) - v sin(theta) in ball
  arithmetic from the EM complex ball zeta = u + iv.
- A2 MAJOR [measured]. The planned EM remainder policy (first omitted
  Bernoulli term x kEmSafety = 4, monotone-checked) is not a theorem for
  s = 1/2 + it. The enveloping property that justifies first-omitted-term
  bounds holds for real s > 0; on the critical line the classical rigorous
  bound is Backlund's (Edwards, Riemann's Zeta Function, section 6.4):
  first omitted term times |s+2M+1|/(sigma+2M+1), a factor that grows like
  t/(2M) and reaches ~1143 at t = 20000 with M = 8. Reviewer experiments at
  cost-minimal N (just above t/2pi, where the monotone check passes) measured
  true-remainder/first-omitted ratios of 3.06 (t=300), 7.49 (t=1000), 26.6
  (t=5000), 66.2 (t=20000): the factor-4 policy under-reports by up to ~17x
  while every runtime check passes. The monotone check detects only that the
  series has not passed its divergence turnover; it bounds nothing. No
  document pins N. With N >= t the measured ratio drops to ~1.03 and the
  cost is trivial at these heights; the sound fix is Backlund's constant
  computed as a ball plus a pinned N policy.
- A3 MAJOR (partially recorded as O1). The D1 theta remainder bound is
  empirically calibrated, not proven, and the safety factor is load-bearing,
  not headroom: the true remainder strictly exceeds (c7 + c8/t^2)/t^13 at
  every height (the omitted tail is a sum of positive terms; measured ratio
  1.0000000175 at t=200). MATHS.md's "tight-zone ratio <= 1.000000" is a
  %.6f print artefact, and the suite never checks tight_max against
  kThetaSafety at all. O1 has no owning stage, yet stage 6's verifier is
  specified to trust "published theta bounds": that premise is false until
  O1 closes. A naive Stirling sector bound will NOT close it (sector
  constants at these orders far exceed 4); it needs Gabcke's critical-line
  constants or an equivalent derivation.
- A4 MAJOR [verified]. The coefficient provenance chain is broken. The
  committed discovery tool, run verbatim, outputs wrong constants
  (c_5 = 334/795137; c_6 = -85/9062, wrong value AND sign; garbage c_7+)
  while printing a success-looking cross-check (|series-ref| = 1e-93 at
  t=1e9, insensitive by construction: the same overfit failure mode D1a
  itself diagnosed). Root cause: the acceptance loop takes the first
  limit_denominator cap with relative error < 1e-6, so a spurious
  small-denominator convergent wins before the true denominator's cap is
  tried. MATHS.md D1a and DECISIONS.md say the caps were "extended/fixed";
  the committed tool does not reproduce the production table. Meanwhile the
  classical closed form that generates the entire table in one line appears
  nowhere in MATHS.md. Replace discovery with the derivation; keep the
  script, if at all, as a numerical confirmer with a self-test asserting it
  reproduces kCRat.

### B. Unsound code in approved or shipped surfaces

- B1 MAJOR [verified by inspection + reviewer demo]. CBall::mul sets the
  radius to L1(a) x L1(b), which includes the full centre product magnitude:
  every product ball contains zero by construction. Sound, but useless for a
  certification engine; any Z assembled through it is permanently Contested.
  The correct bound needs only the deviation cross terms
  (centre x radius + radius x radius) plus the centre rounding at wp.
- B2 MAJOR [verified by inspection + reviewer demo]. CBall::mul_real omits
  the centre-rounding term (the centres are directed-rounded with no radius
  compensation: exact inputs times a rounded coefficient produce a
  false-exact zero-radius ball off the true value) and drops the
  cfr x ball-radius cross term; its radius arithmetic also uses plain
  round-to-nearest double products outside the outward primitives.
- B3 MAJOR [verified by inspection + reviewer demo]. CBall::add omits the
  centre-rounding term entirely: two exact same-precision inputs whose sum
  needs p+1 bits yield a zero-radius ball around a rounded centre.
- B4 MAJOR [verified by experiment]. Ball::parse derives its radius from the
  double image of the centre: parse("1e-320", 128) returns radius exactly 0
  (false-exact; true parse error ~3.7e-359), and parse("1e400", 128) returns
  radius 9.88e-324 against a true representation error of ~1.5e361, a
  non-enclosure by ~684 orders of magnitude. Root cause partly B6. An
  approved stage-2 public API violating the stage-2 attack-3 ledger item
  (false-exact), which was only ever probed for up_mul.
- B5 MAJOR [verified by experiment]. Ffix::mul silently wraps for legal
  in-range operands: from_int(2^32).mul(from_int(2^32)) returns raw 0 with
  err = 1 and no exception, against the file's own "products beyond range
  throw rather than wrap silently". The operand guard admits products up to
  2^240 that the three-limb assembly cannot represent. The test generator,
  despite an "adversarial magnitudes" comment, never exceeds 2^36 raw.
- B6 MINOR [verified by derivation]. half_ulp_bound returns exactly 0.0 for
  every subnormal argument (denorm_min * 0.5 rounds to zero under
  ties-to-even), silently defeating the "never a false-exact zero" invariant
  for any caller with subnormal centres.
- B7 MINOR. convolve_cyclic performs no input validation; inputs near 2^64
  silently produce wrong residues. cb::fabs_upper takes fabs after a
  directed conversion, which rounds negative values toward zero (magnitude
  under-estimate) before the fabs.

### E. Plan-level defects

- E1 MAJOR. The cost model prices the wrong algorithm. The (3/2)(D/H0)
  transfer assumes work density ~ t^{1/2} (naive RS per point), but section
  4b commits to Hiary's amortised T^{1/4+o(1)} multipoint method, whose
  density exponent gives a different share factor and ~3 orders of magnitude
  less per-point work at H0. The table therefore prices neither
  Platt-Trudgian's marginal cost nor zetaforge's expected cost, and it
  asserts exactly the exponent that pending D4 is supposed to derive.
  Related: the derivation-defining anchor "7.5M core-hours" carries no
  pinpoint citation anywhere in the repo, and the dollar conversion silently
  assumes a term-throughput constant (~1.6e8 certified term-ops per
  core-second) that no benchmark yet supports; $0.01/core-hr is a spot vCPU
  (SMT thread) price, not a physical-core price.
- E2 MAJOR. The longest pole is stage 5, and it is understated: no published
  work supplies a certified-error analysis of the amortised multipoint
  method (Odlyzko-Schoenhage and Hiary are complexity results, not certified
  implementations). D3, D4, D5, D8 are all pending; zeta/, certify/, the
  Turing machinery and the certificate format do not exist. The programme's
  hardest open mathematics is between stages 4 and 5, not at the campaign.
- E3 MAJOR. The gating protocol depends on an external reviewer no document
  defines, and the entire gate history to date closed within ~32 hours,
  a cadence incompatible with independent expert review. The money gates
  (7, 9, 10) have no minimum soak time. There is no wall-clock timeline
  anywhere in the plan, no bus-factor note, and no competition register.
- E4 MAJOR. The signal-ladder claim is currently untrue: stages 2 and 3
  closed with no tag, no release, no packaged artefact (git tag count: 0),
  against STATE.md's own inherited obligation.
- E5 MINOR. Certificate semantics are unowned: stage 5 emits and stage 6
  verifies a format nobody freezes; stage 9(a-i)'s "diff must be EMPTY" has
  no comparison tolerance semantics; 16 bytes/ordinate cannot represent
  +/-2^-102 absolute at campaign heights without an (unspecified)
  block-delta encoding; the determinism config hash's contents are described
  differently in different documents and omit library versions (MPFR/GMP).
  PLAN.md carries a duplicate "Data volume" section, the stale copy
  contradicting the archival correction. The stage-9 recompute cost quote
  drops the mandated 2x isolation reserve.
- E6 MINOR. CITATION.cff names the author "Misbah Shah", which does not

## 6. Potential

Honest assessment of what this project can be worth if repaired:

- The strongest claim is not the record extension. A 10^11 window is a 3.3%
  extension; defensible, but "any positive extension is a record" is the
  weakest true statement available. The durable value is (a) the first
  OPEN, replayable certificate pipeline with an independent cross-toolchain
  verifier for this problem class, and (b) the released ordinate dataset in
  new territory. Platt-Trudgian produced neither public certificates nor a
  public zero database at their frontier.
- The signal-ladder intuition is right and currently unexploited: a
  certified [0, 10^6] artefact with certificates, a frozen format, and the
  Rust verifier (stages 5+6) is a complete, publishable, independently
  checkable object at near-zero compute cost. It is the natural first
  public release, and most of the project's reputational value concentrates
  there rather than in the campaign spend.
- The engineering culture on display (pre-registered attacks, honest
  retraction records, integer-exact rounding primitives) is itself the
  portfolio signal the plan's section 2 wants; the findings above, if fixed
  in the open with the same discipline, strengthen rather than weaken it.
- v2 ("height 10^13 outright") is priced by the plan's own model at ~$760k
  equivalent and should not be mentioned without an envelope.

## 8. Review method note

Six specialist review passes ran independently with their own scratch builds
and experiments (theta mathematics; EM/RS mathematics; ball arithmetic; test
integrity; protocol and state integrity; plan and strategy), producing 95
raw findings. A planned adversarial cross-verification pass was cut short by
session limits; in its place, every load-bearing claim in this document was
reproduced directly during synthesis ([verified]), or is presented with its
reviewer's recorded measurement ([measured]). Claims that failed
reproduction were dropped.
